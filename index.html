1) Definition, time, aims

Definition: examining the pregnant woman's abdomen by inspectionالفحص بالعين, palpationالجس باليد and/or auscultationالاستماع بالسماعة.

Time: late in pregnancy, once the uterusالرحم is large enough to tell fetal parts apart by touch.

Aims

  • Estimate the period of gestationمدة الحمل.
  • Determine lie, presentation, position, attitude and engagement.
  • Assess fetal well-being: movements and heart sounds.
  • Detect any deviation from normal; assess fetal growth and size.

Indications (when to do it)

  • Every ante-natalقبل الولادة visit.
  • On hospital admission for any reason, and generally daily during the stay.
  • Before invasive treatment such as amniocentesisسحب عينة من ماء الجنين بإبرة.
  • Before auscultating the fetal heart rate (F.H.R).

Contraindications: placental abruptionانفصال المشيمة عن جدار الرحم and premature laborالولادة المبكرة.

You want to know what is inside a gift box without opening it. You feel from outside: is the toy standing or lying down, head up or down? But if the box is fragile (abruption or early labor), you don't press on it at all.
الفحص بيتم بالنظر واللمس والسمع في آخر الحمل عشان نعرف وضع الجنين وصحته. مينفعش يتعمل لو في انفصال مشيمة أو ولادة مبكرة.

🩺 Nursing connection

  • Why it matters: the findings guide the plan of care. A breechالمقعدة لتحت or transverseعرضي baby near term must be reported to the physician, who decides the management (for example turning the baby from outside, or cesarean).
  • Timing: the grips are usually performed from about 28 weeks, and the Pawlik grip waits until about 36 weeks. This is general nursing practice; use your lecturer's wording in the exam.
  • Why abruption is a contraindication: pressing on the uterus may worsen the separation and the bleeding. Warning signs: a painful, hard "board-like" uterus, with or without vaginal bleeding, and a changed fetal heart rate. Do not palpate; monitor vital signs and F.H.R and call the physician.
  • Why premature labor is a contraindication: handling the uterus can stimulate contractions.
  • Bleeding rule: with any vaginal bleeding, never do a vaginal examination until placenta previaالمشيمة تغطي فتحة عنق الرحم is ruled out.
  • Ethics: explain, get consent, protect privacy and dignity, and document everything.
الفحص بيوجّه الخطة التمريضية: أي وضع غير طبيعي (مقعدة أو عرضي) قرب الولادة نبلّغ الدكتور. في انفصال المشيمة أو الولادة المبكرة منلمسش البطن، ولو في نزيف مفيش فحص مهبلي قبل استبعاد المشيمة المنزاحة.

2) The six key terms (most important for the essay)

Fetal Lie

Relationship of the fetus's long axis to the mother's long axis: longitudinalطولي, transverseعرضي or obliqueمائل.

A pencil in a pencil case: along the case, across it, or tilted.

Attitude

Relationship of fetal parts to each other. Flexionالثني: chin on chest, arms crossed over thorax, thighs flexed over abdomen, back convex, knees bent. Opposite: extensionالرأس مفرود للخلف.

A cat sleeping curled like a ball is flexed. A cat stretched out is extended.

Presentation

The part of the fetus at the cervical osفتحة عنق الرحم: vertexقمة الرأس، الرأس لتحت, breechالمقعدة لتحت, or face, brow, shoulder.

The first kid standing at the classroom door, ready to go out.

Position

Relationship of the fetal denominatorنقطة الإشارة to the maternal pelvis, in 3 parts: (1) left or right, (2) denominator: occiputمؤخرة الرأس for vertex, sacrumالعصعص for breech, mentumالذقن for face, scapulaلوح الكتف for shoulder, (3) anterior, posterior or transverse. LOA = left occiput anterior. ROT = right occiput transverse. OP = occiput against the mother's spine.

Like an address: street (left/right) + landmark (occiput) + floor (front/back).

Engagement

The widest diameter of the presenting part has passed through the pelvic inletمدخل الحوض. For the head this is the biparietal diameterالقطر بين عظمتي الجمجمة الجانبيتين = 9.5 cm. Checked by abdominal or vaginal examination.

A ball whose widest part has dropped through the hoop: it is stuck in and won't wobble.

Station

Level of the lowest part of the presenting part relative to the ischial spinesنتوءان عظميان في الحوض. At the spines = 0. Range -5 to +5. Above the spines is negative (head 2 cm above = -2); below is positive (1 cm below = +1).

An elevator: the ischial spines are floor zero. Above = basement floors (negative), going down = positive.

Note: the lecture slide wrongly says "above the spines is positive". The correct answer is negative.

Lie = الجنين واقف ولا نايم بالنسبة للأم. Attitude = متكور ولا مفرود. Presentation = أول جزء عند فتحة الرحم. Position = ظهر الجنين ناحية أنهي جنب (شمال/يمين وقدام/ورا). Engagement = الراس دخلت الحوض وثبتت. Station = الراس نزلت قد إيه بالنسبة لنقطة الصفر.

🩺 Nursing connection

  • Lie: at term it should be longitudinal. A transverse or oblique lie cannot deliver vaginally and carries a risk of cord prolapseنزول الحبل السري قبل الجنين when the membranes rupture. Report it, and if her water breaks call for help immediately.
  • Attitude: full flexion presents the smallest head diameter to the pelvis, so labor is easier. Extension presents larger diameters and can prolong labor.
  • Presentation: about 95% of term babies are vertex. A breech needs physician planning for the birth.
  • Position: occiput anterior is the most favorable for labor. Occiput posterior (OP) often causes severe back pain and a longer labor; the nurse can encourage position changes such as hands-and-knees and give back massage.
  • Engagement: in a first pregnancy the head usually engages about 2 weeks before labor (lighteningنزول البطن لتحت وتخفيف الضغط على الصدر); she breathes easier but urinates more often. A head still floating at term in a primigravida should be reported, as it may suggest a small pelvis or malpresentation.
  • Station: with cervical dilatationاتساع عنق الرحم it shows the progress of labor and is plotted on the partographرسم بياني لمتابعة تقدم الولادة.
كل مصطلح له أثر تمريضي: الـlie العرضي خطر نزول الحبل، الثني الكامل بيسهّل الولادة، الـOP بيسبب وجع ضهر فنغيّر وضع الأم ونعمل مساج، والراس اللي لسه عايمة قرب الولادة في أول حمل نبلّغ عنها.

3) Preparation and procedure

Equipment: tape measure, Pinard fetoscopeسماعة جنين غير إلكترونية or sonic aid, client record.

  1. Introduce yourself, explain the procedure, take the woman's agreement.
  2. Keep privacy and wash your hands.
  3. Ask her to empty her bladderالمثانة first.
  4. Position her supineنايمة على ضهرها with one pillow under the head and knees slightly flexed.
  5. Bare the abdomen from the symphysis pubisعظمة العانة to the xiphoidآخر عظمة في القفص الصدري.
  6. Place a small rolled towel under her right hip; stand on her right, facing her.
The rolled towel tilts her a little. Lying flat, the heavy uterus presses the vena cavaأكبر وريد يرجّع الدم للقلب like a foot on a garden hose, so blood from the legs can't return well.

Step 1: Inspection

  • Size: large or small.
  • Shape: a pendulousمتدلي لقدام abdomen in a primigravidaحامل لأول مرة suggests a contracted pelvisحوض ضيق.
  • Scars of previous operations, e.g. cesarean section.
  • Pigmentation such as linea nigraخط داكن من السرة للعانة; striae gravidarumعلامات تمدد الحمل.
  • Hair distribution, edemaتورم, and herniaفتق: بروز عضو من جدار ضعيف.

Step 2: Fundal level

Gives an idea of the duration of pregnancy. Use warm hands. Push the uterus to the middle with the right palm, then move the ulnar borderحافة الكف ناحية الخنصر of the left hand down from the xiphoid until you feel the resistance of the fundusأعلى نقطة في الرحم. Count the finger-breadths between fundus and xiphisternum, or measure with the tape from the symphysis pubis to the fundus in cm. Example: at 24 weeks the fundus measures about 24 cm.

بنجهز الأم (موافقة، خصوصية، مثانة فاضية، نومة على الضهر، فوطة تحت الورك الأيمن)، وبعدين نبص على البطن، وبعدين نقيس ارتفاع الرحم: عدد السنتي من العانة تقريباً = عدد أسابيع الحمل.

🩺 Nursing connection: why each step?

  • Explain, consent, privacy: respects dignity and lowers anxiety. A tense woman tightens her abdominal muscles and makes palpation harder.
  • Warm hands, gentle touch: cold hands trigger muscle tension or contractions. Handwashing is infection control, before and after.
  • Empty bladder: a full bladder is uncomfortable and pushes the uterus up, giving a falsely high fundal height.
  • Knees flexed and pillow: relaxes the abdominal wall.
  • Towel under the right hip: prevents supine hypotension syndromeهبوط الضغط من النوم على الضهر. Warning signs: dizziness, pallor, sweating, nausea, low blood pressure, changed fetal heart rate. Immediate action: turn her onto her left side.

What the inspection findings mean

  • Linea nigra and striae: normal hormonal and stretching changes; reassure her.
  • Edema of the face or hands, or sudden swelling: check blood pressure and urine protein, as it may signal pre-eclampsiaتسمم الحمل: ضغط عالي مع بروتين في البول.
  • Cesarean scar: an important part of the obstetric history; it affects the plan for birth.
  • Fundal height versus weeks: from 20 to 36 weeks the height in cm is roughly equal to the weeks (within about 2 cm). Larger than expected: possible twins, excess fluid (polyhydramniosزيادة ماء الجنين), a big baby or wrong dates. Smaller: poor fetal growth, low fluid (oligohydramniosقلة ماء الجنين) or wrong dates. Report any mismatch.
كل خطوة ليها سبب: المثانة الفاضية عشان القياس يطلع صح، الفوطة تحت الورك عشان متحصلش هبوط ضغط، واليدين الدافية عشان متحصلش انقباضات. ولو في ورم مفاجئ بالوش واليدين نقيس الضغط ونبلّغ، وأي فرق بين سنتي الرحم والأسابيع نبلّغ عنه.

4) The four Leopold maneuvers

The fetus is a toy inside a clothes bag. Grip 1 holds the bag from the top, grip 2 from the sides, grip 3 from the bottom with one hand, grip 4 you turn toward the feet and slide your fingers down.

1) Fundal grip

Purpose: which part is in the fundus (head or breech) and the lie.

How: stand on her right, facing her face. Both hands on the fundus, fingers together, using finger pads.

Head: hard, smooth, globular, mobile, ballottableيرتد ويترجرج لما تزقّه. Breech: soft, irregular, large, less mobile.

2) Lateral (umbilical) grip

Purpose: location of the fetal back and limbs.

How: still facing her face. Palms on both sides at umbilicus level; steady one side and palpate the other, alternately.

Back: firm, smooth, convex. Limbs: small, irregular, mobile, slippery knobs.

3) Pawlik grip (1st pelvic grip)

Purpose: the part in the lower uterine segmentالجزء السفلي من الرحم: size, mobility, degree of descent. Done at the 9th month (36 weeks).

How: right hand on the symphysis pubis, thumb parallel to the right inguinal ligamentرباط ثنية الفخذ, fingers toward the left. Other hand beside the uterus. Ask her to breathe in and out; as she exhales, bring thumb and fingers together.

Head: hard, smooth, mobile if not engaged, fixed if engaged. Breech: soft, irregular.

4) 2nd pelvic grip

Purpose: whether the presenting part is floatingمتحرك فوق الحوض, fixed or engaged; its position and flexion.

How: face her feet. Both hands on the lower uterus; slide fingertips down toward the pelvic brim while bringing them together.

Interpretation: the cephalic prominenceنتوء الرأس on the side opposite the back = brow, head flexed. On the same side as the back = occiput, head extended.

Afterwards: auscultate the fetal heart and count for 1 full minute, drape the abdomen, record findings and her reaction, replace equipment, wash hands.

المسكة 1: إيه اللي في القاع. المسكة 2: الظهر فين والأطراف فين. المسكة 3 (باولك): إيه النازل وهل انغرس. المسكة 4: الراس مثنية ولا مفرودة. أول تلاتة بتبصي في وش الأم (المسكة 3 بتبصي لها برضه حسب المحاضرة)، والرابعة ناحية رجليها.

🩺 Nursing connection

  • Technique: palpate between contractions with warm, flat hands and gentle pressure, and ask about pain. Stop and report severe pain, a rigid uterus, bleeding, or a uterus that does not relax between contractions.
  • Grips 1 and 2: together they confirm the lie and show where to place the Pinard or Doppler: over the fetal upper back.
  • Grip 3 (Pawlik): the breath-out relaxes the abdominal wall so you can feel the presenting part. Ask her to breathe gently and not hold her breath.
  • Grip 4: helps tell if the head is engaged and flexed. A non-engaged head in a primigravida at term should be reported.
  • Limits: findings can be unreliable with obesity, excess fluid or an anterior placenta; an ultrasound confirms when you are unsure.
  • Report immediately: breech or transverse lie at 36 weeks or later, a head still floating at term, or a size that does not match the weeks.

Documentation example

"36 weeks. Fundal height 36 cm. Longitudinal lie, cephalic presentation, back on the left, head not engaged. F.H.R 140 b/m, regular, heard in the left lower abdomen. Mother comfortable, no contractions. Physician informed."

Common mistakes in the lab exam

  • Forgetting consent, handwashing, or an empty bladder.
  • Standing on the wrong side, or not facing her feet in grip 4.
  • Pressing hard with fingertips instead of gentle flat pads.
  • Counting the F.H.R for less than a full minute.
  • Not draping, not documenting, or not washing hands at the end.
الأهم في الامتحان العملي: تعملي المناورات بالترتيب وبلطف، وتبلّغي عن أي وضع غير طبيعي، وتكتبي النتائج بالشكل اللي في المثال، وتتجنبي الأخطاء الشائعة المذكورة.

5) Fetal heart rate (F.H.R)

Normal range 120 to 160 beats/min (the lecture also lists 110 to 160 among reassuring signs). Below 120 = bradycardiaبطء القلب. Above 160 = tachycardiaسرعة القلب.

The baby's heart is a tiny clock. Ticking normally is fine; too slow, too fast or uneven means tell the doctor.

Using the Pinard stethoscope

  1. Clean the abdominal piece with an alcohol wipe.
  2. Place the bell over the fetal back (found with the grips), press the head plate against your forehead for bone conduction, and move until the sound is loudest.
  3. Remove your fingers from the stethoscope and keep pressure with your forehead.
  4. Palpate the mother's radial pulseنبض الرسغ to confirm you are not hearing her own pulse. If synchronized, try another location.
  5. Count the baseline rate for 30 to 60 seconds between contractions.

Reassuring: 110 to 160, regular rhythm, accelerationsارتفاع مؤقت في النبض. Non-reassuring (notify the physician or midwife): unexplained tachycardia or bradycardia for 10 minutes or longer, irregular rhythm, gradual or abrupt decrease in rate.

بنحط السماعة على ظهر الجنين ونعد النبض دقيقة. الطبيعي 120 لـ160. وبنقيس نبض الأم الأول عشان منسمعش نبضها بدل الجنين. أي حاجة غير طبيعية بنبلغ الدكتور.

🩺 Nursing connection

  • Objectives from the lecture: diagnose pregnancy; confirm the position and presentation; show the fetus is alive; diagnose fetal distressمعاناة الجنين من نقص الأكسجين or fetal death in the uterus; diagnose multiple pregnancyحمل توأم (two separate heart sounds with different rates); follow the progress of labor.
  • Which device: the Pinard is usually audible from about 18 to 20 weeks. A Dopplerجهاز سونار صغير بالصدى or sonic aid can detect the heart earlier, about 10 to 12 weeks, and uses gel (clean the probe after use).
  • Possible causes of tachycardia: maternal fever or infection, dehydration, anxiety, anemia, some drugs, or fetal hypoxiaنقص الأكسجين.
  • Possible causes of bradycardia: cord compressionضغط على الحبل السري, supine hypotension, too strong or too frequent contractions, placental abruption, or rapid fetal descent.
  • If the F.H.R is non-reassuring: help her onto her left side, check her vital signs, notify the physician or midwife, and be ready for measures ordered by the physician or protocol, such as oxygen by mask, IV fluids, and stopping oxytocin if it is running. Keep re-checking and record each reading.
  • Record: time, rate, rhythm, relation to contractions, maternal position, fetal movements, and who was informed.
السماعة بتأكد إن الجنين عايش وبيتحمل. لو النبض مش طبيعي: نخلي الأم على جنبها الشمال، نقيس علاماتها الحيوية، نبلّغ الدكتور، ونجهز للإجراءات اللي بيأمر بيها زي الأكسجين والمحاليل، ونكتب كل قراءة.
